# Story treatment: the house, Nacre and the passages

Status: **proposal** (2026-10-25), a story and level-design pass for the Unreal line after *The
Stormworks Escape*. Nothing here is built. The decisions in section 1 change existing canon and
need the owner's approval. Characters already established are in
[CHARACTER_BIBLE.md](CHARACTER_BIBLE.md) and [STORY_CONTINUITY.md](STORY_CONTINUITY.md).

## 1. Canon kept, and the decisions you need to make

**Kept as is:**

- **Mara:** the dry, practical Saltmere mechanic who trusts calculations more than people, with her
  toy-like look, goggles, scarf and Ivo's canister.
- **Ivo:** Edda's apprentice, who knew Mara as a child and gave her the first pressure gauge she
  ever took apart. He has kept the safety systems alive in secret.
- **Sorn:** a frightened, reasonable-sounding warden who believes the low town "was always going to
  flood".
- **Edda's spiral**, the Watch, and Breakwater as a tide-engine prison.
- **The tether and its brass rings** (built). They stay Mara's tool in every world. The new reason
  is a strong one: **Ivo installed those rings as maintenance points throughout the passages.**

No design for "the house" exists in the repository. It is treated as new and placed carefully.

**Decisions needed, each with my recommendation:**

1. **The new premise against the grounded mystery.** Recommendation: *keep both*.
   - **The world:** the tide engine moves water between worlds, and Edda's governor is the network's
     regulator. Her spiral is the key pattern every passage opens to.
   - **Sorn's diversion** becomes the large-scale version of his habit: the stored pressure of a
     whole prison network, sent down the Saltmere channel.
   - **Canon's answers survive:** why Mara was taken, why the machines answer her drawings, and why
     Ivo knows her all stay true.
2. **Pip against Nacre.** Two nonverbal companions would compete, and Pip is designed but unbuilt.
   Recommendation:
   - Retire Pip from this arc.
   - Give Pip's secret (a needle set to point home) to the small object Nacre steals.
3. **Ivo's status.** In canon he hides inside Breakwater by choice. Proposed change: the lit lamp
   gave him away. **Sorn takes him during the storm Mara just escaped**, which turns Ivo's note into
   a last message rather than a plan.
4. **The note says "take the tide at dawn."** Proposed change: she leaves at once, because the Watch
   boats are already searching.
5. **The planned Pier Arcade chapter is absorbed by the Theater.** The theater's mechanical
   performers are Edda's automata, and her recorded voice is found there. The web chapters (the
   village and the lighthouse climb) stay as the web line. The Unreal campaign runs from Stormworks
   straight to the house.

## 2. Premise and treatment

**Premise:** A mechanic who only trusts what she can calculate follows a missing friend into a
house that opens onto other worlds. She learns that the safest way out is to make sure everyone else
can get home too.

**Treatment:**

- **The house.** Mara crosses the storm to Ivo's old cottage under Knoll Rock and finds it warm,
  familiar, and wrong. Something under the floor steals Ivo's old gauge. The house folds open, and a
  huge octopus, **Nacre**, drags her into the flooded passages under it. She believes she's being
  attacked. In fact it's pulling her away from Sorn's lamps.
- **The network and the deal.** Under the house lies a conservatory where whole houses hang like
  lanterns, each window opening on another sky. This is the junction of a network Ivo and Edda built
  for travel and trade. **Sorn turned it into a prison:** he locks each world's circulation (water,
  wind, rain, stories) and spends the pressure to hold his cells shut. Ivo is held at the network's
  centre. Nacre has been holding the damaged connections together alone. **The deal grows by
  actions, not words:** Nacre knows the passages, and Mara can go where it can't.
- **The five worlds.** Each restores one circulation, and its release carries her into the next:
  - she frees the currents of the Sunken Conservatory;
  - she frees the winds of the Orchard Above the Rain;
  - the rain fills the Glasswater Rapids;
  - the rapids wake the Theater of Borrowed Hours, where she rewrites Sorn's single approved ending
    and finds Edda's voice and Ivo's truth;
  - in the quiet Sea of Unsent Letters she understands the danger: opening Ivo's cell carelessly
    would send all the stored pressure back through the worlds.
- **The choice.** She chooses to restore a return route before she frees him.
- **The return.** The sea drains toward a spiral door beneath the house. Back in the transformed
  house, she recombines everything she has learned. Sorn closes every route; she opens one he didn't
  know existed. She frees Ivo, who helps finish the escape. The pressure flows home instead of down
  the Saltmere channel.
- **The end.** A quiet, playable morning in a house that can now go anywhere.

## 3. Character arcs

| | Wants | Fears or flaw | How it shows in play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mara** | Out, with Ivo | Trusting what she can't measure; other people's problems as "scope creep" | Early kindnesses are optional and small. The world shows their effects later (channel workers open a safer route). In the Sea, **restoring the return route first is a required act that delays her own goal.** The recurring gesture moves from recoiling (cinematic), to accepting (player), to reaching first (player: she **fires the tether at Nacre's offered tentacle** to make a living anchor). |
| **Ivo** | Mara safe, and the network mended | Guilt: he built what Sorn misused | His rings and chalk "IV" in every world; an unsent letter in the Sea; Edda's script annotated in his hand in the Theater. Once freed **he acts:** he holds the counterweight so Mara can steer the final lift, then mends the last passage with her. |
| **Nacre** | To hold the passages together until someone can repair them | Sorn's lamps, the light that locks; being seen as a monster | It starts urgent and clumsy, and is misread. It ends as a partner that finally rests once the house can hold itself. **It never solves challenges and never hints unless the player looks at it.** |
| **Sorn** | Everything in its assigned place: people, winds, rain, stories | Uncertainty; being the warden who lost Breakwater | He's shown before he speaks: labelled cages, stencilled regulations ("RAIN: HOLD UNTIL REQUIRED"), a theater that allows one ending, Watch lamps that sort and file. **In the climax he closes routes and Mara opens them.** He isn't destroyed: he's left on Breakwater's crown, "his assigned place", watching the tide go out the right way. |

**Nacre's signals** (taught by repetition, always paired with a sound, never with text):

| Signal | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Pearl-white, curled tentacle tip | "Come" |
| Flattens and turns deep blue | "Hide: a lamp" |
| Inks a spiral in the water | "A passage is here" |
| Three taps on glass | Its greeting, which becomes Mara's too |
| Coral flush and looking away | Embarrassed or pleased |

## 4. Chapters

| Chapter | Movement | Local objective | Emotional shift | Standout moment | What causes the next transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The House (arrival) | Walking, then a forgiving steered descent | Find Ivo's clue | Comfort → suspicion → terror → astonishment | The tentacle hiding behind a teacup | The house folds into the flooded passage |
| Sunken Conservatory | Swimming and currents (plus the tether) | Restore the circulation | Wonder → confidence | Redirecting the Mouth, the great intake | The freed stream carries her and an air bubble up; water becomes cloud |
| Orchard Above the Rain | Gliding (seed sail) | Uncage the winds | Freedom → exhilaration | Diving through the storm-cage as it opens | The released rain falls; she follows it down |
| Glasswater Rapids | Sliding on a gate panel | Catch the transport before the floodgate | Exuberance | The water wakes the theater's wheel | The transport is diverted into the theater; she follows |
| Theater of Borrowed Hours | Parkour and rhythm on moving sets | Interrupt the single performance | Cleverness → revelation | **She gives the play a new ending; the backdrop opens onto a real sea** | The stage becomes a boat |
| Sea of Unsent Letters | Boating and reading reflections | Reach the spiral door safely | Calm → trust → resolve | Steering while Nacre holds a passage open | The sea drains toward the door under the house |
| The House That Learned to Move | Short recombined sequences | Free Ivo without breaking the worlds | Tension → release → home | She reaches first; she and Ivo turn the last lock together | A quiet playable morning; she chooses when to step outside |

**The learned ideas carry forward:**

- reading currents prepares players to read wind;
- the rapids' racing lines prepare them for the theater's choreography;
- the theater's cues prepare them to read reflections.

**Each world's tether role is small and specific:**

- **Conservatory:** rings on drifting glass anchor her across a current.
- **Orchard:** a ring pulls a cage door.
- **Rapids:** a ring swings a counterweight.
- **Theater:** rings on the scenery pull a set piece into place.
- **Sea:** she tethers the boat to Nacre.

Swimming, gliding, sliding, parkour on moving sets and boating are **planned, not built** in Unreal.
Mara already has swimming clips; the tether, jumping and checkpoints are built.

## 5. Detailed playable sequence: the house through the Sunken Conservatory

Key: **P** = player control, **C** = brief cinematic (skippable; what a skip must still convey is in
brackets), **T** = teaching, **R** = recovery or restart point, **O** = optional, **♪** = sound.

**Arrival**

1. **P, R:** the storm-lashed jetty under Knoll Rock, the cottage window warm. ♪ The full storm, plus
   a lighthouse hum carrying Ivo's motif (four notes, the spiral's rhythm).
2. **P:** the door. ♪ **The storm muffles the instant she crosses the threshold**: a low-pass, rain on
   the glass, a clock, kettle-tick creaks. The house has its own sound identity.
3. **P, T:** the workbench she remembers. Ivo's brass gauge sits where he always left it. Inspecting
   it (E) shows the spiral on its dial. Mara: "He kept it." The needle twitches toward the floor.
4. **O, three details (any order, none required):**
   - **Funny:** a kettle steams sideways toward the drain, as if being sniffed.
   - **Comforting:** Edda's and Ivo's pencil marks of child-Mara's height on the doorframe. Mara
     stops; her hand rises to the mark.
   - **Wrong:** water in a glass climbs up the side, and her reflection in the window turns its head
     late.

   ♪ For each, the house sound drops out for a beat. **Under the floor something copies her
   footsteps one beat late.** It stops when she stops, and plays once more when she does.

**The tentacle**

5. **P:** a tentacle slides out of the sink drain and takes the gauge. ♪ Wet suction, a pleased little
   exhale.
6. **P, T:** the comedy of being looked at.
   - **Looked at:** when the camera centres on it, it freezes and retreats; looking away lets it
     creep.
   - **Hiding:** chased, it hides behind a teacup far too small, then a lampshade.
   - **The trick:** aiming the tether ring-marker at it makes it flinch. **This teaches that the
     tentacle reacts to her attention.**

   Mara: "That is not a pipe."
7. **P → C:** the Watch searchlight crosses the windows. The tentacle flattens and turns deep blue:
   **signal 2 ("Hide: a lamp") seen first, not yet understood.** The house begins to shake. ♪ The
   storm briefly returns through the cracks; a Watch horn sounds far off.
8. **C (3 s, skippable):** tentacles burst from every drain and grab Mara. A bubble of air wobbles
   over her head as she's dragged under. [If skipped: Nacre has grabbed her, and she can breathe.]
   ♪ Outside sound cuts to nothing, then her own heartbeat and the bubble's rubbery wobble.

**The descent**

9. **P, T, forgiving:** the hallway stretches downward. She's being towed, and she **steers around**
   floating doors, chairs and photographs. A hit only nudges; there is no failure. **It teaches the
   swimming input** (left stick to steer, shoulder to dodge up and down) inside a moment that can't
   be lost.
10. **P:** the hall folds into a flooded shaft lit by glowing seeds. Nacre's grip loosens. ♪ Distant
    Watch lamps hum above; their light goes past the shaft and misses her. A fair player sees the
    danger they were pulled away from.

**The reveal**

11. **P → reveal (P):** she swims out into the **Sunken Conservatory**. Houses hang like lanterns,
    and every window opens onto a different sky. The camera stays in her control; a gentle look-at
    pulls toward the largest lantern for one second. ♪ The home motif played on glass bells, slow,
    underwater-wide.
12. **P:** Nacre's whole shape shows briefly, in silhouette. It holds out the gauge. Players who take
    it see Nacre flush coral and look away. ♪ Three soft taps on glass: its greeting. Mara: "...You
    could have asked." **(Gesture 1: she flinched in step 8; here she takes the object, not the
    tentacle.)**

**Learning to swim, safely**

13. **P, T, R:** Glass Room 1 is sheltered and has no oxygen limit (the bubble holds). Free swimming
    among seed-lights:
    - **Delight:** bubble-farmer creatures tend bubbles like plants; brushing one makes it pop a tiny
      bubble at her.
    - **Escape:** the room's exits are cracked tubes with weak currents.
14. **P, T:** the first current pushes her back; she learns to swim across it. A second current
    carries her along, and riding it feels fast and good. **Rule taught: currents run along the
    visible streams of seeds.**
15. **O, kindness:** the gardener. A stubborn snail-like gardener is losing seedlings to a current.
    Mara herds three of them back by swimming upstream of them, which practises current-reading.
    - **The response:** the gardener plants a seedling *on Mara's head*, inside the bubble. It
      travels with her for the chapter and blooms when circulation returns.
    - **Cost:** cheap and funny.
16. **P:** the problem made visible. Sorn's intake clamps choke the main channel. Stencilled sign:
    "CIRCULATION SUSPENDED BY ORDER". Dead currents, dimmed seeds. Nacre inks a spiral by a valve
    (signal 3).

**Puzzle and escape**

17. **P, T, R:** the puzzle uses currents as tools. Open three valves in sequence to route one
    current, so it carries her (and a drifting glass pane) to a tether ring she can anchor to.
    **Moves used: swim, ride, anchor.**
18. **P, R: the Mouth.** A great regulator intake works like a hungry mechanical creature: it
    **inhales on a visible rhythm**, every 4.2 s like the engine's breath (canon).
    - **Read the rhythm:** she swims between the inhalations.
    - **Redirect the pull:** she pulls three baffle plates with the tether so the pull swings away
      from the gardens.
    - **Win** by watching and moving. A caught swim only drags her back to the last baffle (**R**).
    - ♪ A rising suction, a creak, then a satisfying clang as each baffle locks.
19. **P, the escape:** the Mouth reverses, and the rush of water chases her up a widening shaft. It is
    exciting but fair: wide lanes and currents in her favour.

**Restoration and exit**

20. **P → continuous camera:** circulation returns. Seeds brighten, the gardener's seedling blooms on
    her head, and Nacre turns pearl-white. Nacre offers a tentacle at the edge of a rising stream:
    **gesture 2, and the player can choose to accept it.** Swimming into the offered tentacle hands
    her into the stream. ♪ The home motif on glass and water.
21. **P (steering only), uncut:** a rising stream lifts her and a giant air bubble, and **the water
    becomes cloud**. ♪ All the sound releases into open air: wind, a bird, silence.

    *The Orchard begins on the cloud.*

## 6. Three short scenes

**The first encounter (step 6).** The tentacle holds the gauge up like a prize. Mara turns. The
tentacle freezes, then lowers the gauge behind a teacup, curling itself after it as if that makes it
invisible. A tip pokes out, checks, and goes back.

**Mara:** "I can see you."

It slowly slides the teacup to cover a bit more of itself.

**The first trust (step 20).** The current is too strong. Nacre's tentacle stretches across it, tip
curled, white. The first time, in the house, she recoiled. Now the player steers into it, or not;
there's another route that takes longer. If she takes it, the grip is careful, almost formal. Nacre
flushes coral and looks away.

**Mara (quietly):** "Thank you, old girl."

It's the same line she gives machines that finally work (canon). Nacre doesn't know what it means
and taps the glass three times anyway.

**The reunion (final).** The last lock is a spiral. Ivo is behind it, older than his voice, sleeves
rolled up and calm.

**Ivo:** "You took the long way."
**Mara:** "Someone had to fix your network."

She can't turn the lock alone; it takes two hands at the far ends. **Ivo grips one end.** The player
turns the other in the spiral's rhythm, the motif from step 1. Nacre's tentacle wraps both their
wrists. The pressure goes home, not down the channel.

**Ivo (to Nacre):** "Seven years. You held."

Nacre flushes coral.

**Mara:** "Don't. It gets embarrassed."

## 7. The five biggest risks, with corrections

1. **Scope.** Six worlds and six movement modes is far beyond a small team (Stormworks alone took
   two passes).
   - **Correction:** ship the house and the Conservatory as a vertical slice first, and only
     greenlight later worlds after a human playtest.
   - Build every world from **one modular "glass room" kit** plus a few world-specific pieces.
   - Keep chapters to 15–25 minutes.
   - If needed, merge the Rapids into the Orchard's descent (slide down a rain channel) and the Sea
     into the final house.
2. **Nacre is expensive** (a rigged octopus, tentacle physics, expressive colour).
   - **Correction:** most of the time show **only tentacles and eyes**.
   - Make the tentacles procedural spline chains (the project already simulates cables).
   - Express mood with a colour or emissive parameter on one material plus the eye.
   - Show the full body only in silhouette or in two cinematics.
3. **Underwater rendering on this laptop.** The render thread already runs 22–27 ms and the GPU about
   21 ms in Stormworks.
   - **Correction:** fog colour and density, one cheap caustics light function, very few shadowed
     lights.
   - Make the lantern-houses low-poly emissive stand-ins.
   - Measure every room with the self-test's per-segment frame costs before adding detail.
4. **Lore and tone confusion.** Dimensions plus tide engines plus a governor can easily turn into
   explanation.
   - **Correction:** one rule players can feel: *"water, wind and rain travel between worlds; Sorn
     locks the doors."*
   - Show it through the spiral door, the stencilled orders and restored circulation.
   - No exposition scenes; Nacre stays nonverbal; Edda's voice is heard only in the Theater.
5. **A companion that solves things or nags, and derivative house surrealism.**
   - **Correction:**
     - Nacre gestures only with signals the player already knows, and hints only when the player
       looks at it.
     - It never acts in the player's place and never enters the camera arm.
     - The house's strangeness stays rooted in our own engineering imagery (circulation, valves, the
       spiral, the tide's breath) rather than toy scale or relationship counselling.

## 8. The smallest first slice

**Steps 1–12 only: the house arrival through the gauge being returned in the reveal.** About 6–8
minutes.

**What to build:**

- **The house:** one cottage set, reusing the workshop kit and lighting.
- **The three details:** the climbing water, the late reflection, the copied footsteps.
- **The tentacle:** one actor with procedural spline animation and a look-away rule.
- **The grab:** one 3-second cinematic.
- **Swimming:** basic swimming in a water volume (Mara already has swim clips; swimming is **not
  built in Unreal**).
- **The descent:** a towed corridor with floating obstacles.
- **The reveal:** a fog-lit reveal room with a few lantern-houses.
- **Sound:** the doorway muffle, the underwater cut, Nacre's sounds.

**What it must answer with real players** (not the bot):

- Do they laugh at the tentacle before they fear it?
- Do they understand afterwards that it saved them?
- Do they *want* to swim toward the lanterns?

If those three land, the direction is worth the chapters. If not, the problem is in the slice,
cheaply.
