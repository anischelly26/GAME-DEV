# Character bible

Status: **proposal** (2026-10-17) for the cinematic overhaul. Mara's look is built; Pip is designed
here and built in Phase C; Ivo and Sorn are voices until later chapters. The story behind each is
in [STORY_CONTINUITY.md](STORY_CONTINUITY.md). Until real voice performances exist, characters
speak through subtitles, expressive animation and mechanical sounds, never placeholder speech.

## Mara Venn (player)

- **Role:** tide-engine mechanic from Saltmere; the player.
- **Look (built):** an original toy-like figure, 1.40 m, big head with a living face (eyes, lids,
  brows, mouth, moods), ochre work jacket with brass buttons, a belt and pouch, teal trousers,
  boots, auburn hair in two buns, brass goggles pushed up, a coral scarf, Ivo's brass steam
  canister on her back.
- **Personality:** intelligent, sceptical, stubborn, dryly funny, practical; trusts calculations
  more than people; kind in deeds before words. Gradually more open.
- **Voice (subtitles):** short sentences, technical precision used as humour, understatement
  under pressure.
  - "Fresh copper. Nobody fixes anything in a prison. Somebody fixed this."
  - "Thank you, old girl." (to a machine that finally works)
  - "You'd better know where you're going." (to Pip)
- **Never:** cheerleads ("We did it!"), explains the puzzle she just solved, narrates what the
  player can see.
- **Body language to animate:** hands on hips when assessing a machine; a small fist pump on a
  hard-won success; folded arms while waiting (built); a wince and a laugh at a big fall.

## Pip (companion, new)

- **What it is:** a small brass-and-glass creature built from a forgotten pressure gauge, with a
  salvaged Watch lamp for an eye. Ivo made it from scrap to find Mara; it was damaged on the way.
- **Look:** a round copper gauge body (about 25 cm across) with the gauge face as its "belly";
  one large glass eye on top, lit amber, with an iris shutter for expressions; two retractable fan
  fins either side that let it hover in short hops; three tiny spring legs; a little steam vent
  that puffs when it is emotional. Original silhouette: a floating teapot-sized gauge, not a
  robot head.
- **Moves:** hops on its legs; flutters (fins out) for a few seconds at a time; clings to Mara's
  canister when scared or tired; spins its gauge needle when thinking.
- **Personality:** curious, mischievous, dramatic, overconfident, easily frightened, unexpectedly
  clever, loyal once trust is earned.
- **Wants:** to find Ivo, its maker; to see daylight over the sea (it was built in the dark).
- **Fears:** the Watch (its eye came from one; it is afraid of being "taken back"); deep water.
- **Secret:** its gauge face carries Edda's spiral; Ivo set its needle to point the way home.
- **Mistakes:** leads Mara the wrong way with total confidence; startles a Watch at the worst
  moment; slips off things.
- **Voice:** no words. Chirps, clicks, whistles, steam puffs, needle twitches, eye shutters.
  Subtitles render its meaning in brackets only when the meaning is not obvious, e.g.
  "[Pip, very sure:] ►►►" or "(Pip flatly refuses.)".
- **Gameplay role (limited, clearly signposted):** lights dark corners (its eye); fits into small
  spaces to trip remote latches; distracts a Watch for a few seconds (it flies past its lamp and
  hides); notices Ivo's traces (its eye flares at chalk marks). It never solves a puzzle by
  itself, never blocks Mara, never enters the camera's arm, never breaks stealth on its own.
- **Arc:** frightened stowaway (Ch1 ending) → reckless guide (Ch2) → partner who chooses to stay
  in the deep water with Mara (Ch3) → keeper of a memory it did not know it had (Ch4).

## Ivo Brannock (ally)

- **Role:** Edda Venn's apprentice on the governor; imprisoned seven years for sabotaging the
  diversion; keeps the prison's old safety systems alive in secret.
- **Presence so far:** chalk "IV" marks, the repaired pipe, the steam kicker, intercom lines.
- **Personality:** resourceful, guarded, conflicted, dry, tired; protective of Mara in a way he
  will not explain at first.
- **Voice:** clipped, technical, sometimes tender by accident.
  - "Follow the service lights. Don't follow me."
  - (recording) "Mara. You still draw the spiral in the margins. Your mother did too."
- **Reveal plan:** repairs → intercom → recordings (Ch3) → seen at a distance → met in person
  (Ch4 or later).

## Warden Sorn (antagonist)

- **Role:** Breakwater's warden; architect of the flood diversion.
- **Personality:** controlled, persuasive, unsettling; reasonable in tone, certain in purpose.
- **Motivation:** save Breakwater (and the people in it) from the next great spring tide by
  sending the surge down the Saltmere channel; fear of being the warden who lost the island.
- **Presence:** recorded announcements, security broadcasts, work orders, visible consequences
  (sealed doors, neglected safety lamps, the abandoned village), a figure on the crown at the end
  of Chapter Three; a personal offer later.
- **Voice:**
  - "Containment breach, tier three. Inmate Venn will be returned unharmed. The tide does not wait,
    and neither do I."
  - "Every engineer thinks her numbers are the truth. Mine have three thousand people living
    behind them."

## The Watch (enemies)

- **What they are:** steam automata in warden's caps, running on rails (built). One lamp eye.
- **Readable states (built and to extend):** patrol (lamp sweeping), suspicious "?" (stops,
  stares, lamp narrows), alarm "!" (horn, red lamp), chase or tether, search (lamp sweeping toward
  the noise), stunned "z" (head lolls, steam leaks), back to patrol.
- **Personality through behaviour:** officious and literal. They announce rules ("Inmate out of
  bounds. Halt!"). They are slightly comic when stunned or confused, never cute.
- **Rules (fair, documented in GAME_DESIGN.md):** sight cone and range, solid cover blocks sight,
  tall grass hides, noise draws them, a ground pound stuns them, a catch returns Mara to the
  checkpoint and is not a fall.

- **The lighthouse keeper's child**: never met; the drawings in the village ("Papa keeps the light").
