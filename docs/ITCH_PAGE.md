## Description

itch's editor is rich text: paste from `description_to_paste.txt`, not from this table version.

---

**Mara Venn woke up inside Breakwater, the prison that runs on the tide. She is getting out.**

It Only Needs Mara is a short early demo of a single-player 3D adventure. Play it from the start and it runs
straight through both parts.

**The Stormworks Escape.** Ivo's workshop, a storm, and his maintenance tether. A teal brass ring means
the tether works: pull machines into place, swing across the gaps, and run the old catwalk as it falls
behind you.

**The house under Knoll Rock.** Ivo's cottage, under the lighthouse. His gauge is on the bench, the
storm goes quiet at the door, and something in the drains has noticed you. Look at it and it stops.
Look away and it creeps on. It is not a pipe.

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move, look | WASD, mouse | Sticks |
| Jump; swim up | Space | A |
| Swim down | C or Left Ctrl | LB |
| Tether, interact | E or left click | Right trigger |
| Skip a cinematic | Space, Enter or E | A or B |
| Back to the last checkpoint | R | View / Back |

### Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with a DirectX 12 graphics card. Built and tested on a laptop with an RTX 2050
and 8 GB of RAM, at about 45 frames per second. Close other heavy programs first. Windows may warn
about an unknown publisher: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

This is a prototype: the art is rough and it is still being built. Tell me what confused you, where you
got stuck, and whether the tentacle made you laugh.
